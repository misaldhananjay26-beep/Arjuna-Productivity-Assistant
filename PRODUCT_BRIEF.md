# Product brief

## User
A student founder or independent builder who wants one calm place to decide what matters next, capture ideas, prepare meetings, and keep momentum.

## Promise
Arjuna helps turn a busy day into one clear next step.

## MVP boundary
This first public version is local-first. Tasks, notes, meetings, and focus sessions live in the browser. AI is optional and only uses the deployed Gemini endpoint when `GEMINI_API_KEY` is configured.

## Safety boundary
No silent access to Gmail, WhatsApp, calendars, contacts, or private files. Any future external integration must be explicit, scoped, and confirmation-gated for sending, accepting, deleting, or changing data.
